# Classroom artist inventory

Inspected all four uploaded photos: IMG_1051.jpeg, IMG_1052.jpeg, IMG_1053.jpeg, IMG_1054.jpeg. There are **51 distinct named artist cards**. Overlapping photos were deduplicated. Student work above and in front of the cards was not treated as another named artist. The original classroom photos are not redistributed in this project.
