# School content review

Reviewed 18 November 2026: all **566 original images** (524 artworks and 42 portraits), plus every replacement. **92 original images excluded; 83 approved replacement works added.** Current collection after the 19 November teacher revision: **502 artworks and 42 portraits across 50 artists**. Three selected galleries contain nine works; 47 contain at least ten.

## Applied selection rule

School Grade 7/8: no nudity (including classical/religious anatomy and nude infants), violence, visible injuries/blood, weapons, sexual content, or drug/tobacco imagery. Ambiguous images excluded conservatively. Ordinary clothed portraits, non-graphic historical context and abstract non-anatomical figures remain.

Reviewers inspected actual local reproductions in labelled contact sheets and opened ambiguous or complex works individually. This includes background figures, small sculptures within paintings, text within an artwork, portraits, and main featured images. No work was blurred, covered or censored to pass; replacement files preserve their source composition. Architecture photographs show the identified architectural subject.

Excluded files have been removed from the current deployable `dist` folder and current downloadable project archive, not merely hidden by the interface. Older Git commits and browser caches can still contain previous editions. The original Grade 1 project is separate and was not changed by this review.

The JSON manifest records every approved image’s SHA-256 fingerprint and each excluded image/reason. Deployment tests reject an unreviewed or changed image, an excluded image fingerprint, or an extra unlisted raster file in `dist/assets`. Automated tests enforce the recorded review; they do not perform visual recognition or certify school-board approval.

Non-graphic historical discussion may still address inequality, conflict and social context. Read-aloud, quizzes, chronological gallery ordering and attribution remain. Student-facing links do not lead out to image searches or unrestricted web pages.

## Future additions

Visually inspect the entire image at its published resolution, including background figures and written text. Do not assume a classical, museum-held or educational artwork passes the rule. Add its exact filename, approval reason and SHA-256 to the review manifest; update credits, coverage and feature records; then run the tests. If suitability remains uncertain, leave the image out.
